Hungary — Deep Dive

The most fellowship-rich country for US-based REECA scholars in Europe
Country Note

Hungary is currently the most active in Europe in terms of funded fellowship programs explicitly accessible to American scholars. MCC, HIIA, and Corvinus CIAS all have live programs. Politically contested context — research accordingly and go in with eyes open.

🏛️ Academic & Research Institutions
Mathias Corvinus Collegium (MCC)
mcc.hu/en

Largest multidisciplinary education and research institution in Central Europe. Budapest Fellowship Program (10-month, fully funded for American scholars), Visiting Fellowship, and teaching opportunities. 7,800+ students, 24 locations.

HungaryFellowship
HIIA — Hungarian Institute of International Affairs
hiia.hu

Foreign policy think tank. Runs the Future Leaders Program (3-month fellowship for early-career IR professionals, Sep–Dec). Posts research positions. Apply at HIIA website.

HungaryFellowshipThink Tank
Danube Institute
danubeinstitute.hu

Conservative-leaning Budapest think tank. Host institution for Budapest Fellowship Program fellows. Occasionally posts research associate positions.

HungaryThink Tank
Corvinus University — CIAS
uni-corvinus.hu

International research hub of Corvinus University. 5- or 10-month visiting fellowships for Junior and Senior Researchers. Applications open for 2026/2027. Excellent academic environment.

HungaryFellowship
IAS CEU (Budapest Open Society Fellowship)
ias.ceu.edu/fellowships-ias-ceu

Institute for Advanced Study at CEU. 2025/26 theme: "Modalities of Resilience and Resistance: Academia and Society in Illiberal Times." Junior fellows €1,500/month, Senior €2,600/month.

HungaryFellowship
Cold War History Research Center (Budapest)
coldwar.hu

Under the auspices of University of Budapest. Provides organizational framework for Cold War research in Hungary. English-language output.

Hungary
🏆 Fellowships Specific to Hungary
Budapest Fellowship Program
hungaryfoundation.org

Hungary Foundation + MCC. Fully funded 10-month program for young American scholars/professionals. Research, teaching at MCC, immersion in Hungarian culture. Applications open in spring each year.

HungaryFellowship
Hungarian Studies Association
aseees.org

ASEEES-affiliated. Furthers research in Hungarian history, politics, and cultural affairs. Formed 1970. Good for networking with Hungarian studies scholars.

HungaryNetwork
Hungarian Historical Review
hunghist.hu

Published by Hungarian Academy of Sciences Research Centre for Humanities. Open access. Good submission target for Central European history work.

Italy — Deep Dive

Strong philological tradition · Underused by American REECA scholars
Country Note

Italy has a genuinely strong and underused Slavic studies tradition, especially for Russian literature and philology. The AIS and its Mappa della Slavistica give you instant access to every active Slavic scholar in Italian academia. The EUI in Florence is a gateway to EU-funded postdoctoral work.

🏛️ Primary Organization & Research Network
AIS — Associazione Italiana degli Slavisti
associazioneslavisti.com

Founded Rome 1971. Promotes Slavic studies in Italy. Publishes Studi Slavistici journal. Their Mappa della Slavistica is a live database of every Slavic studies scholar at Italian universities — essential for networking. National congress every 5 years.

ItalyNetwork
Studi Slavistici (Journal)
fupress.com

AIS flagship journal. Scientific articles, bibliographic updates, reviews. Open access. Good submission target for Italian-facing Slavic research.

ItalyJournal
Europa Orientalis (Journal)
europaorientalis.it

Salerno-based. One of Italy's oldest and most respected Slavic studies publications. The editorial network is itself a community of Italian Russianists and comparativists.

ItalyJournal

Slovakia — Deep Dive

Punches above its weight for policy & memory studies · Underused by US scholars
Country Note

Slovakia is dramatically underused by US-trained REECA scholars — which means less competition for positions and fellowships. SFPA, GLOBSEC, and ÚPN are high-value entry points. Also the gateway to V4 networks and Visegrad Fund eligibility.

SFPA — Slovak Foreign Policy Association
sfpa.sk

Ranked among the world's top 100 think tanks for defense and national security. Posts research and junior analyst positions. Strong V4 + Russia/Eastern Europe coverage.

SlovakiaThink TankJobs
GLOBSEC Policy Institute
globsec.org

Among the best think tanks in CEE for foreign affairs. Annual GLOBSEC Forum is a major event. Research fellowships and analyst roles posted regularly.

SlovakiaThink TankFellowship
ÚPN — Institute of National Remembrance
upn.gov.sk

Public archival and scientific research center for Slovakia's period of non-freedom 1939–1989. Runs conferences, research calls, and international projects. Essential for communist-era history.

SlovakiaResearch Inst.
Slovak Academy of Sciences (SAV)
sav.sk

Primary publicly funded research organization in Slovakia. Individual institutes for history, literature, and social sciences post research positions and fellowships.

SlovakiaJobs
Collegium Carolinum (Munich)
collegium-carolinum.de

Affiliated with LMU Munich. Focuses on history and culture of the Bohemian lands, Slovakia, and Czechoslovakia in European context. Key German hub for Czech/Slovak studies.

SlovakiaNetwork
Slovak Studies Association
slovakstudies.org

ASEEES-affiliated. Promotes interdisciplinary research related to the Slovak experience worldwide. Small but valuable for making contacts in Slovak academic circles.

SlovakiaNetwork
Carpatho-Rusyn Research Center
carpathorusynresearch.org

Very niche. History and culture of Carpatho-Rusyns at the Slovak-Ukrainian borderlands. Useful if your work touches minority cultures of the region.

SlovakiaNetwork
IVO — Institute for Public Affairs
ivo.sk

Slovak civil society and democracy research. Occasionally posts research positions. Good for alt-ac paths.
